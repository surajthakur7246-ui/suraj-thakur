FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

What is your typical project timeline?
+

Project timelines vary based on scope. A standard motion graphics video takes 1–2 weeks. Short film VFX can take 3–6 weeks. Full 3D animation projects typically require 6–12 weeks. I provide a detailed timeline breakdown in every project proposal.

What is your minimum project budget?
+

My minimum engagement starts at $500 for simple editing projects. VFX and animation projects typically start from $2,000+. For complex productions, I provide custom quotes based on scope, deliverables, and timeline. Quality is always prioritized over shortcuts.

Do you work with international clients?
+

Absolutely. I work with clients globally via remote collaboration. I'm comfortable with all time zones and use tools like Frame.io, Notion, and Slack for seamless communication. I accept payments in USD, EUR, GBP, and INR.

What software and tools do you use?
+

My primary toolkit includes After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Blender, Cinema 4D, Houdini, Nuke, Photoshop, and Illustrator. For game cinematics, I work with Unreal Engine 5. I'm constantly learning new tools to stay at the industry's cutting edge.

What is your revision policy?
+

Standard projects include 2–3 rounds of revisions. I work iteratively with clients and provide progress updates at every milestone. My goal is to deliver exactly what you envision — client satisfaction drives everything I create.

Do you sign NDAs and contracts?
+

Yes, absolutely. I sign NDAs for confidential projects and provide professional contracts for all engagements. I use clear agreements that protect both parties and outline deliverables, timelines, and payment terms clearly.
